⎘ Zoom integration

Connect your Zoom account

Link your Zoom account so that when someone books a meeting on your Calendo page, a Zoom meeting is created automatically in your own Zoom account and the join link is emailed to you and your invitee.

✓Zoom connected.
Connect Zoom
  1. Click Connect Zoom and approve access on Zoom.
  2. Set any meeting type's location to Zoom in your dashboard.
  3. New bookings on that meeting type get a Zoom link automatically.
