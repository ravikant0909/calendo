What it does

Calendo is a scheduling platform. With the Zoom integration connected, whenever someone books a meeting on your Calendo booking page for a meeting type set to Zoom, Calendo creates a Zoom meeting in your own Zoom account and includes the join link in the confirmation emails to you and your invitee. Each Calendo user connects their own Zoom account — meetings are never created in a shared account.

Permissions requested: meeting:write:meeting (create the meeting) and user:read:user (read your Zoom account email/ID to show which account is linked).

Adding the app

Prerequisites: a free Calendo account (sign up) and a Zoom account.

  1. Log in to Calendo and go to Connect Zoom (also reachable from Settings → Integrations, or Availability → Calendar settings).
  2. Click Connect Zoom. You'll be redirected to Zoom's authorization screen.
  3. Review the requested permissions and click Allow.
  4. You'll return to Calendo with a "Zoom connected" confirmation showing the linked Zoom account.

You can also install Calendo from its Zoom App Marketplace listing — choosing Visit Site takes you to the Connect Zoom page above.

If a step fails, see Troubleshooting below.

Using the app

Generate Zoom links for booked meetings

Prerequisite: Zoom connected (see above).

  1. In Calendo, open a meeting type (Overview → a meeting type, or Meetings).
  2. Set its Location to Zoom and save.
  3. Share that meeting type's booking link. When an invitee books a time, Calendo creates a Zoom meeting in your account and emails the join link to both of you. The meeting also appears in your Zoom account's upcoming meetings.

Check which Zoom account is connected

Prerequisite: logged in. Go to Connect Zoom — it shows the connected Zoom account's email, or a Connect button if none is linked.

Removing the app & your data

Disconnect from within Calendo

  1. Go to Connect Zoom (or Availability → Calendar settings).
  2. Click Disconnect Zoom.

Remove from your Zoom account

  1. Sign in at marketplace.zoom.us → Manage → Added Apps.
  2. Find Calendo and click Remove.
What de-authorization means & how your data is removed: when you disconnect in Calendo or remove the app from your Zoom account, Calendo deletes your stored Zoom data — your encrypted Zoom OAuth access/refresh tokens and the connected account's email/ID. Removing the app from Zoom also triggers Zoom's deauthorization webhook, which Calendo handles by deleting that data and confirming deletion back to Zoom. After removal, "Zoom" meeting types will simply stop generating Zoom links (existing bookings are unaffected). Deleting your entire Calendo account also deletes all Zoom data.

Troubleshooting

  • "Zoom connection failed" after authorizing: start the connection from inside Calendo (Connect Zoom), not from the Zoom Marketplace "Add" button — Calendo's flow needs to begin on its own site.
  • No Zoom link on a booking: confirm Zoom is connected and that the meeting type's Location is set to Zoom.
  • Need to switch Zoom accounts: click Disconnect Zoom, then Connect Zoom again and authorize with the other account.
  • Still stuck? Email support@calendo.dev.
