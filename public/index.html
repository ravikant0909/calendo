Features

Everything you need to schedule smarter

Simple enough to start in minutes, powerful enough to handle any scheduling workflow.

AI-Powered Booking

Your invitees chat naturally: "I need 30 minutes sometime next week." The AI checks your calendar, suggests times, and books.

Calendar Sync + Conflict Detection

Two-way sync with Google Calendar and Outlook. Auto-detect busy times. Holiday blocking. Never double-book.

Team Scheduling

Round-robin assignment with priority weighting. Collective scheduling for group availability. Built for teams of any size.

Payments & Coupons

Charge for consultations via Stripe. Offer discount codes. Accept payments before or after booking confirmation.

Email Sequences

Automated reminders, follow-ups, and thank-you emails. Customizable templates with variables. Set it once, forget it.

Analytics & Export

Track bookings, cancellations, no-shows, popular times. Export CSV reports. Routing form conversion analytics.
